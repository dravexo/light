🇬🇧 Playing with VPN (For Restricted Regions)

Note: PUBG Lite is banned in some countries including India. However, you can use a VPN (Virtual Private Network) to play the game by connecting to a server in another country.

Steps to Play with VPN:

  • Step 1: Download a reliable VPN app from Google Play Store (e.g., ExpressVPN, NordVPN, Turbo VPN)
  • Step 2: Open the VPN app and select a server from another country (e.g., USA, Singapore, Australia)
  • Step 3: Connect to the VPN until you see "Connected" status
  • Step 4: Open PUBG Lite app and log in to your account
  • Step 5: Keep the VPN connected while playing the game

⚠️ Important Notes:

  • Always keep VPN connected while playing the game
  • Choose a VPN with fast internet speed and stable connection
  • PUBG Lite is banned in India, so using VPN is necessary
  • Some VPNs are free but Paid VPNs are faster and more stable
